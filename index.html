Our process, your advantage

From idea to execution

Five steps from the first call to final files. You always know what happens next and when you will see work.

01

Discover

Research & goals

A free call to understand your business, your users and the problem. We look at competitors and what your audience already responds to.

02

Plan

Scope, flows & calendar

A written scope with deliverables, timeline and a fixed quote. Then user flows and wireframes, a keyword map, or a content calendar, depending on the service.

03

Create

Design, edit, write, post

High-fidelity screens in Figma, edited videos, SEO articles and designed posts. Shared with you as they are made, not at the end.

04

Review

Feedback rounds

You comment directly on the work. Revision rounds are included in the price, so we keep going until it is right.

05

Deliver

Handoff & support

Developer-ready Figma files, final videos, published content and a report. On a retainer, the next month's plan starts here.

Ask us on LinkedIn
What services does UXpert Solutions offer?+

Four: UI/UX design for apps and websites, video editing, SEO content writing, and social media management. You can hire us for one or combine them.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Yes. We take on clients in the US, UK, Europe, Australia, the Philippines and the Middle East, through Upwork or directly. Calls and updates are scheduled around your time zone.

How long does a project take?+

A landing page or a batch of posts usually takes about a week. A full app design takes three to six weeks depending on the number of screens. You get a timeline with the quote.

Are your designs developer-ready?+

Yes. We hand over organised Figma files with components, spacing and assets, and stay available to answer your developer's questions.

Do you offer monthly plans?+

Yes. Social media management, video editing and SEO content are available as monthly retainers, and design can be added to the same plan.
